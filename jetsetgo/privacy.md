# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 6, 2025

Jet Set Go ("the Game") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection practices.

## Information Collection

**We do not collect any personal information or data from users of Jet Set Go.**

The Game does not:
- Collect personal information
- Track user behavior
- Store gameplay data
- Use analytics services
- Employ cookies or tracking technologies
- Share any data with third parties

## Data Storage

The Game does not store any user data on external servers. Any game progress or settings are stored locally on your device only.

## Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit: https://blog.willishq.com
